# Runbook: Privacy and Deletion Orchestration

## Procedure

1. Authenticate the requester and create a global privacy/deletion case with a
   human reference and country checkpoints.
2. Identify authorized country memberships and applicable retention/legal holds.
3. Execute cell-local anonymization/deletion for operational data while retaining
   legally required financial/trip evidence in minimized form.
4. Revoke sessions, social links, push tokens, safety shares, and active service
   channels according to the approved lifecycle.
5. Remove or anonymize private objects and provider data where permitted.
6. Require idempotent completion evidence from every cell, then close the global
   case with an audit record and requester notification.

Do not directly drop legacy support/privacy tables until zero references, empty
ownership, completed migration, and reviewed retirement evidence are proven.
