# ADR 000: Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decision owners:** Architecture and affected domain owners
- **Related systems:** List the affected boundaries

## Context

Describe the problem in plain language. Include current behavior, scale and
reliability pressures, security/privacy constraints, and why a decision is
needed now.

## Decision drivers

- What must remain true?
- What risk is unacceptable?
- What operational skill and tooling already exist?
- What must remain backward-compatible?

## Decision

State the choice and its scope. Separate what is implemented now from later
migration work.

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative A

Explain its strengths, why it was not chosen, and when it might become a better
choice.

## Consequences

### Benefits

- Concrete outcome.

### Costs and risks

- Concrete tradeoff plus its mitigation.

## Security, privacy, and compliance

Describe changed trust boundaries, data classes, authorization, retention, and
audit effects.

## Reliability and operations

Describe failure modes, alerts, recovery, rollback, and the affected runbook.

## Validation

List tests, metrics, controlled failure exercises, and acceptance criteria.

## Follow-up

- [ ] Implementation or migration item
- [ ] Documentation/runbook update
